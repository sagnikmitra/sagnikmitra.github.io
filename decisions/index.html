The questions an AI should ask you back

An agent built a SIP tracker. Then it wrote down every call it had made that nobody told it to make, and asked. Each question carries what the system does today, why it does that, what it costs, and a recommendation with its reasoning. You answer. It exports a JSON and a markdown you hand straight back.

That is the whole loop.

Every answer above now has a recorded decision, a chosen option, and a reason. Export it, hand the markdown back to your agent, and the flow diagram updates with each rule pointing at the question that produced it. See what that looks like.

A demo of the Decisions half of a human-to-AI communication framework, part of sgnkai. The SIP tracker, its numbers and its failures are synthetic and self-consistent. Answers are stored in your browser only. Flow
